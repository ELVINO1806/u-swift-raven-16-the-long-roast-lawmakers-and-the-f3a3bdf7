## Entry

This program writes a single satirical sentence to `/output/entry.txt`. The joke is aimed at the people who write sweeping laws for algorithms, crypto, and the internet, yet cannot perform the most basic computer task themselves: the authority is real, the competence is not, and the punchline is the gap between the two.

The whole roast fits in one sentence on purpose. It opens by granting the lawmakers their power — they pass laws on things they cannot explain — and then undercuts it with a small, specific humiliation: an hour lost to a file format, rescued only by an intern. The bigger the law, the smaller the task they fail at, and that contrast is the entire joke.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
